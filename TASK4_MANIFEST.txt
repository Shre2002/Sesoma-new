- Duration per sample: 2.2 seconds
- Total frames: 11,000
- Total duration: 550 seconds (~9.2 minutes)

- ToF sensors: 4 (floor-mounted, upward-facing)
  * tof0 x=-0.20, tof1 x=-0.10, tof2 x=+0.10, tof3 x=+0.20
  * Spatial resolution: 8×8 per sensor
  * Depth range: 0.0–3.74 m, mean 2.27 m
  * NaN rate: 2.62%

- Radar: 1 (A121-like, 60 GHz)
  * Bins: 290 (range resolution 0.02 m)
  * Format: Complex IQ
  * Magnitude range: 0.000006–0.050702

LABEL STRUCTURE
- is_wrong: boolean (correct/incorrect squat)
- wrong_type: string (one of 5 types, null for correct)
- severity: float [0.228, 0.897], mean 0.656
- severity_bin: int [0, 5] (6 bins)
- movement_type: "squat" (100%)
- patient_id: integer
- duration: float (motion duration in seconds)
